## LESSONS LEARNED

- **`Range.getBoundingClientRect()`** on text gives **line-box** bounds, not **glyph ink** bounds. For a flex-centered inner `<span>`, range rect ≈ span box → vertical “offset” reads ~0 and no correction applies. **Canvas `TextMetrics.actualBoundingBoxAscent` / `actualBoundingBoxDescent`** are needed to place the visual center of caps relative to the line box.
- **`transform: scale(A) translateY(B)`** applies **translateY first** (right-to-left composition), then scale. Correction is in **pre-scale** px; scale amplifies layout error and correction together — **do not** divide offset by `sy` for that reason (earlier `/ sy` was wrong).
- **`transform-origin` percentages** as a font-metric fix are brittle (span vs table needed different values; browser/rendering variance).

## PROCESS IMPROVEMENTS

- On **font changes** for components that **JS-scale text**, preflight can flag **metric-aware centering** as a risk so planning includes ink measurement or `text-box-trim` (when support allows) up front.

## TECHNICAL IMPROVEMENTS

- **None required.** If `text-box-trim` / `text-box-edge` become universally available, a future pass could reduce reliance on Canvas for Latin-only cases; mixed-script cartouches may still need measurement or composite strategies.
